Google Business Profile for Businesses: How Local SEO and Google Maps Work

1 September 20269 min readCodehouse Team
The short answer: Google Business Profile is the foundation for helping people find your business in Google Search and Google Maps. But simply having a listing is not enough: it needs the right category, consistent details, genuine services, useful photos, authentic reviews and a website that clearly explains what you offer and where.

If you have a local business, you have probably wondered “how do I appear on Google Maps?” or why a competitor appears more often when someone searches for your service. The answer is not one hidden setting or repeating your city in every sentence. It is a combination of accurate data, relevance, location, user experience and trust.

Google Business Profile for a business and its website work best together. The profile helps someone find you, check your hours or call. The website can explain services, answer questions and lead to a quote request or booking. This guide shows how to connect them without exaggeration or artificial keyword stuffing.

What is Google Business Profile?

It is the public business listing that may appear in Google Search and Google Maps. Depending on your category and information, users can see your name, area, phone number, hours, website, photos, services, reviews and directions.

It is not just an online business card. It is a decision point. Someone searching “accountant Athens,” “café near me,” “Santorini airport transfer” or “web design Thessaloniki” may compare several listings before calling. If details are incomplete or contradictory, the business loses an important part of the first contact.

How does local SEO work?

Local SEO in Greece is the work of making a business easier to understand and more useful for searches connected to a location. Google considers relevance to the search, distance from the user or searched area, and prominence/trust. None guarantees a particular position.

Relevance

The category, services, description and content should explain what you actually do. A restaurant, physiotherapist and website design company should not use the same generic profile. Clear information helps both the algorithm and the customer.

Distance

Google considers the relationship between the search and the business location. You cannot appear everywhere simply by adding many cities to your copy. If you serve areas without a physical base there, list service areas according to the rules and provide genuine information on your website.

Prominence and trust

Reviews, mentions, links, consistent details and genuine activity create credibility signals. There is no safe shortcut with purchased reviews or excessive keywords. Local SEO is a marathon of consistency, not a one-day trick.

What to enter correctly in the profile

A good Google Business Profile is complete without being wordy. Use the business’s official name, choose the most accurate primary category and add secondary categories only when they describe real activities.

  • Name: use the actual brand, not a list of keywords such as “Best Cheap Accountant Athens.”
  • Category: choose the one that best describes the business’s main purpose.
  • Description: explain naturally what you offer, whom you serve and where you operate.
  • Services: add genuine services with short descriptions, not variations of the same phrase.
  • Hours: update them for holidays, seasonal periods and operating changes.
  • Phone and website: check they work on mobile and lead to the right destination.
  • Area: list your location or service areas as they really are.

The role of the website in local SEO

A Google profile has limited space. A website can be a reliable source where visitors learn more. A good service page should answer what you offer, who it is for, which areas you serve, what the process includes and what the next step is.

Keep the key details consistent everywhere: business name, phone, area, hours and website. This does not mean copying the same text in every place. It means Google and customers should not see three different phone numbers or different service descriptions.

More organic visibility also requires a good title, descriptive URL, headings, useful answers and internal links. The business SEO guide explains the general SEO foundations, while the guide to a website that does not bring customers helps you assess what happens after the click.

Reviews and photos: what really helps

Reviews are useful because they reduce uncertainty. Ask real customers for feedback after a completed service without telling them what to write. Respond politely and thoughtfully to positive and negative reviews. Do not reveal personal information or promise something the business cannot deliver.

Photos should show the real premises, product, team, work or process. A clear, well-lit photo is more useful than a generic stock image. Keep files lightweight so the page and profile load comfortably on mobile.

7 common Google Business Profile mistakes

  1. Wrong primary category: choosing a general category that does not describe the main service.
  2. Inconsistent details: different phone number, hours or address on the website, Maps and social channels.
  3. Keyword-stuffed claims: excessive repetition sounds unnatural and may violate rules.
  4. Outdated profile: old photos, wrong hours and services no longer offered.
  5. No website: customers cannot find detailed information, a portfolio or a convenient form.
  6. No review process: satisfied customers do not know how to leave authentic feedback.
  7. Measuring only views: visibility is not the final goal; measure calls, clicks, enquiries and visits.

Local SEO checklist for a business

  • Is the profile verified and the primary category correct?
  • Are the name, phone, area and hours consistent with the website?
  • Are services described in natural phrases customers understand?
  • Does the website link point to a relevant page rather than an unrelated homepage?
  • Are there recent, real photos and up-to-date details?
  • Can you measure calls, directions, forms or requests?
  • Do you respond to reviews without automated or aggressive replies?

Local SEO works best when the profile, website and actual customer experience tell the same accurate story. Frequently asked questions about Google Business Profile and local SEO

What is Google Business Profile?

It is a business profile that appears in Google Search and Google Maps, with a name, category, area, hours, phone number, photos and reviews.

How do I appear on Google Maps?

You need a verified, correctly completed profile, consistent business details, a useful local page on your website and genuine activity that helps Google understand your location and service.

Is Google Business Profile enough without a website?

It may cover the basics of a very small presence, but a website offers more space for services, FAQs, portfolio, content, forms and organic search.

How long does local SEO take?

It depends on competition, area, profile condition, website, reviews and consistency of activity. Local SEO needs ongoing review.

What should I write in Google Business Profile services?

List real services using language customers use, with short and accurate descriptions. Avoid unrelated or excessive keywords.

Should I buy reviews?

No. Reviews should be authentic and come from real customers. Buying or fabricating reviews harms credibility.
