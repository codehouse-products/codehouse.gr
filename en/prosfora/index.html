Offer terms

In plain language — as it should be.

  1. What the no-upfront-cost build includes: Design and development of a business brochure website (business introduction, services, photos, contact form/details, Google Maps and social media links). E-commerce shops and more complex requirements receive a separate proposal.
  2. The monthly subscription covers hosting, domain, SSL certificate, backups, technical maintenance and reasonable content updates (copy, prices, photos, opening hours). The subscription amount is agreed before work begins based on the website’s needs, and will not change without your knowledge.
  3. Minimum term: The offer requires a subscription for at least 12 months. After the first 12 months, service continues month to month and you may cancel at any time with 30 days’ notice.
  4. While you are a subscriber, the website stays online and fully maintained. If the subscription ends, the website is taken offline; by arrangement, you may buy it out and take it with you, including your domain.
  5. The domain: If we register the domain as part of the offer, it is registered to your business. It belongs to you; we do not retain ownership.
  6. Content updates: Reasonable routine updates are included (e.g. new prices, opening hours, photos and announcements). A complete redesign or new sections/features are quoted separately, always at a price agreed in advance.
  7. Delivery time: The first website version is usually delivered within a few business days after we receive your materials (copy, photos and logo).
  8. Offer availability: The offer is for new customers and may be changed or withdrawn for future sign-ups. Existing subscribers retain the terms under which they joined.

The information you provide in the questionnaire is used only to contact you about the offer and is not shared with third parties. See our Privacy Policy.
