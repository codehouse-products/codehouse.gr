  • Pays for itself in months

// How it works

Three steps, no app

The customer sits down, opens their phone camera and scans the table card. The menu appears in seconds, with no app download, account or email required.

  • 1. Scan. Use the phone camera to scan the stand or table sticker.
  • 2. Browse. Categories, photos, prices, allergens and offers in a menu that loads instantly.
  • 3. Order. They know what they want before the server arrives — and often order more.

We design the cards in your venue’s colours, with your logo, QR code and optional Wi-Fi password, ready to print.

// For tourist destinations

One menu, as many languages as you need

In Santorini, Mykonos, Glyfada and other tourist destinations, customers who cannot understand a menu order less and ask more questions. A digital menu addresses this: guests choose a language and see the same dishes in it.

  • Automatic language suggestion based on the visitor’s phone settings.
  • One source of truth. Update a price once and it is updated across all languages.
  • Accurate descriptions, written for visitors rather than translated word for word by machine.
  • No multiple printed menus and less need for staff to explain every dish.

// Before signing elsewhere

Not all QR menus are the same

  1. 01

    Avoid endless subscriptions

    Platforms charging €20–40 per month indefinitely can cost several times more than your own custom menu over two years — and you own nothing at the end.

  2. 02

    Demand speed

    A customer at the table will not wait. The menu should load in one or two seconds, even with a moderate signal.

  3. 03

    Check it on mobile

    All customers will view it on a phone. If a menu was designed for desktop and then squeezed down, it shows.

  4. 04

    Make sure it is yours

    The menu should live on your own domain, not be locked into a third-party account. That way you retain control and your venue’s SEO.

// Frequently asked questions

What owners ask before getting started

What is a digital QR menu?

It is your business menu in digital form. Customers scan a QR code on the table with their phone camera and immediately see the menu, including photos, categories and current prices — without downloading an app.

How much does a QR menu cost?

In the Greek market, a quality custom-designed digital menu typically costs around €200–€600 as a one-off, depending on the number of items, photos and languages. We also offer an option with no build fee and a small monthly subscription covering hosting, domain and updates.

Do customers need to download an app?

No. The menu opens in the phone’s browser within seconds. No installation, account or registration is required — one reason QR menus work in practice.

Can I update prices and dishes myself?

Yes. We provide an easy way to update prices, new dishes and availability. Or send us the changes and we will make them the same day, at no extra subscription cost.

Does it support multiple languages for tourists?

Yes. The menu can be shown in Greek, English and any other language you need, with one-tap switching. It is especially useful in destinations such as Santorini, Mykonos and the Athens Riviera.

Do I also get printed QR table cards?

Yes. We design the cards in your business colours, with your logo, QR code and optional Wi-Fi details, ready to print as a stand, sticker or laminated card.

How long does it take?

A typical digital menu is delivered in 2–5 business days after you send us your items and prices. Menus with many items or dish photography take a little longer.

Can it also support delivery or orders?

The standard menu is for browsing at the table. If needed, we can add buttons for phone orders, WhatsApp, reservations or a delivery platform.

Do I own the menu, or is it locked to a platform?

It is yours. We build it on your domain, not on a third-party platform that charges you indefinitely. If you want to move it later, you can take it with you.

Do you serve areas outside Athens?

Yes. We are based in Glyfada and work with businesses throughout Greece, from Santorini and Mykonos to Thessaloniki. The entire process is remote, by phone and email.
