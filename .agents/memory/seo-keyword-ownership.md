---
name: SEO keyword ownership
description: Non-obvious canonical target decisions for Codehouse commercial keyword clusters.
---

Use one primary URL for each commercial search intent:

- General website construction belongs to `/dimioyrgia-site/`.
- E-shop construction and cost belongs to the existing in-depth e-shop cost article; do not add a parallel commercial landing without a consolidation plan.
- SEO services belongs to `/seo/`; SEO articles remain informational support.
- Free-website informational intent belongs to `/dorean-istoselida/`, while `/prosfora/` is the offer endpoint.
- Do not add a generic Santorini construction landing or retarget an existing Santorini article until Search Console Pages × Queries data identifies which URL already receives impressions.
- A bilingual studio redesign may add broad capability pages, but these support rather than replace the established cost/website-construction targets. Apply the same intent ownership within each language; do not canonicalize complete English translations to Greek pages.

**Why:** The repository already has three overlapping Santorini transfer articles, and a proposed e-shop landing duplicated the existing article's commercial intent during review. The later bilingual editorial redesign required real service pages, not reassignment of established commercial search ownership.

**How to apply:** Before creating or substantially retitling a landing page, map the target query to the current ranking URL. Use internal links to reinforce the selected primary and keep supporting articles informational or case-study focused.
