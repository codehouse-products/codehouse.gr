---
name: Loading film continuity
description: Preserve the live hero film across the loading-to-site reveal.
---

Treat loading as an overlay on the live hero film, not as a separate video presentation. Revealing the site must neither restart playback nor change the film's framing.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that the site continue the video from the exact point reached during loading, with the appearance of uninterrupted playback.

**How to apply:** Keep the same player and composition beneath both states and transition the loading/interface layers only. Preserve the existing playlist and annotated logo. Keep waiting bounded and the site accessible when media playback is unavailable or reduced motion is requested.
