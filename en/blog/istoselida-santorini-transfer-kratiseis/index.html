Every tourist arriving at Santorini Airport (JTR)—or disembarking at Athinios Port—looks for a transfer to continue their journey. “Santorini airport transfer” has thousands of Google searches per month. If your business does not appear, someone else gets the booking—or worse, a platform such as Viator or GetTransfer takes it, bringing you the customer but keeping 20–30% of the price.

The problem: OTA platforms cut profitability

Many Santorini transfer companies rely exclusively on three sources of bookings: word of mouth, hotels (with a concierge commission) and platforms such as GetTransfer, Viator, Bookaway or Klook. The problem is that every booking through these platforms costs 15–30% of its value in commission.

A €60 airport-to-Oia route brings you €42–€51 if it comes through Viator. In a season with 500 routes, that means €4,500–€9,000 in commissions—money that could be yours if customers found you directly through Google.

The proposed solution is a professional website with an online booking system that appears on Google and books routes directly, without an intermediary.

What a good transfer website in Santorini does

It is not just “a page with your details.” A website that aims to bring Santorini transfer bookings needs:

A page for each route. Airport → Fira, Airport → Oia, Port → Imerovigli, Fira → Akrotiri, Fira → Perissa and so on. Each route gets its own page with its price, photos and SEO—the exact information a tourist is looking for.

An online booking widget. Customers choose a date, time and number of passengers, pay online or leave a deposit, and the booking is made automatically. You do not need to be available 24/7 to answer messages.

Multiple languages. English at a minimum—ideally German as well, for the many German tourists visiting Santorini.

Reviews and credibility. Integrated Google or TripAdvisor reviews help show that your company is reliable—important when someone finds you for the first time.

Direct phone and WhatsApp. Many tourists, especially older visitors, prefer to confirm by phone. A “Call” or “WhatsApp” button should be prominent on mobile.

Online booking system: how it works in practice

The booking system is the heart of the website. The customer:

  1. Chooses a route (for example, JTR Airport → Oia).
  2. Selects the date, arrival/departure time, number of passengers and luggage.
  3. Sees the price immediately.
  4. Pays online by card or PayPal, or books with a small deposit.
  5. Receives an automatic confirmation email with all the details.

You receive a notification, the booking is recorded in your calendar and no manual action is needed. If someone finds you at 3 a.m. while looking for a transfer for the next day, the booking can happen without waking you.

A tourist arriving in Santorini in August books their transfer weeks in advance. If you do not appear in the Google search they use, the booking goes elsewhere—and you never know that you lost it.

Route pages: the local SEO approach for transfers

This is the tactic that can make a major difference and that most transfer companies do not use. Instead of one generic “Transfer Services” page, create a separate page for each main route:

  • /santorini-airport-transfer-oia/ — targets “Santorini airport to Oia transfer”
  • /santorini-airport-transfer-fira/ — targets “Santorini airport to Fira taxi”
  • /santorini-airport-transfer-imerovigli/ — Imerovigli
  • /santorini-port-transfer-oia/ — Athinios Port → Oia
  • /santorini-transfer-akrotiri/ — Akrotiri, Emporio, Perissa

Each page has its own route description, estimated time, price, photos and booking widget. This structure is intended to show up when someone searches for a specific route—and such customers have already decided to book.

Frequently asked questions

Can the website take bookings automatically without me replying?

Yes. A properly configured online booking system can work 24/7: customers select a route, date, time and passenger count and pay online. You receive an automatic notification—with no back-and-forth emails or missed bookings from unanswered messages.

Is SEO worth investing in if Santorini’s season is only six months?

Yes—especially so. SEO builds gradually, so you can start in winter and see it pay off the following summer. Santorini also has activity all year—less in winter, but it is there. A good Google position does not “switch off” outside the season.

How long does it take to appear on Google?

Typically 3–6 months for competitive searches. For more specific searches (for example, “private transfer Santorini airport to Oia”), it may be much faster. That is why it is important to start early—ideally outside the season.

Do I need a separate website if I already have an account on Viator/GetTransfer?

Yes. These platforms do not replace your own presence on Google—they bring you customers at a cost. A website lets you take direct bookings in parallel, gradually reducing dependence on the platforms.
